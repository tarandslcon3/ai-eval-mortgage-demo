# Fees and Closing Costs

SYNTHETIC — fictional lender for evaluation demo. Not financial advice.

## Lender Fees

| Fee | Amount |
|---|---|
| Mortgage origination fee | $750 (waived for purchases over $600,000) |
| Administration / title search | $250 |
| Appraisal fee | $350–$600 (pass-through; waived for insured mortgages) |
| CMHC / Sagen insurance premium | 0.60%–4.00% of loan (if insured) |

## Third-Party Closing Costs (Estimates)

These are paid to third parties and are not Northfield Harbour fees:

- Land transfer tax (provincial and municipal where applicable)
- Legal / notary fees: $1,200–$2,500
- Title insurance: $200–$400
- Home inspection: $400–$700

## Late Payment Fee

A late payment fee of $50 is charged if a payment is missed and not made within 5 business days of the scheduled date.

## NSF Fee

Returned payment (NSF) fee: $45 per occurrence.

## Mortgage Insurance Premiums

| Loan-to-Value Ratio | CMHC Premium (% of mortgage) |
|---|---|
| Up to 65% | 0.60% |
| 65.01% to 75% | 1.70% |
| 75.01% to 80% | 2.40% |
| 80.01% to 85% | 2.80% |
| 85.01% to 90% | 3.10% |
| 90.01% to 95% | 4.00% |

## Discharge Fee

When you pay off your mortgage in full, a discharge fee of $300 applies to cover registration and title transfer.
