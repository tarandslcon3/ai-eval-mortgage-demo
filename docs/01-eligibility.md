# Borrower Eligibility

SYNTHETIC — fictional lender for evaluation demo. Not financial advice.

## Overview

Northfield Harbour Lending offers residential mortgage products to borrowers who meet the following eligibility criteria.

## Employment Status

- **Salaried employees**: Must have a minimum of 2 years of continuous employment with the same employer. Probationary employees are not eligible.
- **Self-employed borrowers**: Must provide 2 years of Notice of Assessment (NOA) from the Canada Revenue Agency. Business must be operating for at least 3 years.
- **Contract workers**: Eligible if the contract is in the same field as previous employment and has at least 12 months remaining.

## Credit Score Requirements

- Minimum credit score of 680 for insured mortgages (down payment under 20%).
- Minimum credit score of 620 for conventional mortgages (down payment 20% or more).
- Scores are assessed using the Equifax Beacon 9.0 model.

## Income Verification

All borrowers must provide:
- Last 2 pay stubs (salaried)
- Last 3 months of bank statements
- T4 slips or NOA for the past 2 years

## Residency

Borrowers must be Canadian citizens, permanent residents, or holders of a valid work permit with at least 12 months remaining.

## Age

Borrowers must be at least 18 years of age. There is no maximum age limit.
