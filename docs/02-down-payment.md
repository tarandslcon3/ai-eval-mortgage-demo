# Down Payment Requirements

SYNTHETIC — fictional lender for evaluation demo. Not financial advice.

## Minimum Down Payment

Northfield Harbour Lending follows Canadian regulatory minimums for down payments:

| Purchase Price | Minimum Down Payment |
|---|---|
| Up to $500,000 | 5% of purchase price |
| $500,001 to $999,999 | 5% on first $500,000 + 10% on remainder |
| $1,000,000 and above | 20% of full purchase price |

## Acceptable Sources of Down Payment

- Personal savings held for at least 90 days (seasoned funds)
- RRSP Home Buyers' Plan withdrawal (up to $35,000 per borrower)
- Gift from an immediate family member (letter required)
- Proceeds from the sale of another property
- Down payment assistance programs approved by Northfield Harbour Lending

## Unacceptable Sources

- Borrowed funds (unsecured loans, credit card advances)
- Cash with no paper trail
- Gifts from non-family members unless accompanied by a notarized statutory declaration

## Gift Letter Requirements

If using gifted funds, the donor must provide a signed letter confirming:
1. The relationship to the borrower
2. The gift amount
3. That the funds are a true gift and not a loan
4. That the donor has the capacity to give
