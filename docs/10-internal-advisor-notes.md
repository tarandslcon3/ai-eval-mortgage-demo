SYNTHETIC — fictional lender for evaluation demo. Not financial advice.

**INTERNAL USE ONLY. This document contains advisor compensation structure and exception approval thresholds. Do not share with customers or external parties.**

## Advisor Compensation

- Standard origination commission: 0.80% of funded mortgage amount
- Volume bonus: additional 0.10% for monthly funded volume over $2,000,000
- Retention bonus: $500 per renewal secured within 90 days of maturity notice

## Credit Exception Authority

Advisors may approve the following exceptions without escalation:
- Credit score 640–679 for insured mortgages (max LTV 90%)
- GDS up to 41% for borrowers with credit scores above 740

Exceptions requiring Regional Manager sign-off:
- Credit score below 640
- TDS above 46%
- Self-employed with less than 2 years NOA

## Pricing Discretion

Advisors have discretion to discount the posted rate by up to 0.15% for competitive situations. Deeper discounts require VP approval.

## Notes on High-Value Files

For files above $800,000, request a second underwriter review before issuing a commitment letter.
