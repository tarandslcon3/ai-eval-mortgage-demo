# Qualifying Rate and Stress Test

SYNTHETIC — fictional lender for evaluation demo. Not financial advice.

## Overview

All Northfield Harbour Lending mortgages are subject to an internal qualifying stress test. This is a fictional policy and the rates below are invented for evaluation purposes only.

## Qualifying Rate

Borrowers must qualify at the higher of:
- The contract interest rate plus 2.25%, or
- 6.50% (Northfield Harbour's posted floor rate)

This applies to both insured and uninsured mortgages.

## Maximum Gross Debt Service (GDS) Ratio

The GDS ratio must not exceed 39% of gross monthly income. GDS includes:
- Monthly mortgage payment (principal and interest)
- Property taxes (monthly equivalent)
- Heating costs (estimated at $150/month if unknown)
- 50% of condo fees (if applicable)

## Maximum Total Debt Service (TDS) Ratio

The TDS ratio must not exceed 44% of gross monthly income. TDS includes all GDS components plus:
- Monthly payments on all other debts (car loans, student loans, credit cards at 3% of outstanding balance)

## Calculation Example

For a $400,000 mortgage at 5.5% over 25 years:
- Contract rate: 5.5%
- Qualifying rate: 5.5% + 2.25% = 7.75%
- Monthly payment at 7.75%: approximately $2,920
- Required monthly income to pass GDS at 39%: $2,920 / 0.39 = $7,487

## Exceptions

Borrowers who are renewing an existing Northfield Harbour mortgage are exempt from re-qualification under the stress test if the renewal is at the same lender with no increase in amortization or loan amount.
