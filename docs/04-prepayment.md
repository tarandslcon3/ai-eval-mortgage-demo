# Prepayment Privileges and Penalties

SYNTHETIC — fictional lender for evaluation demo. Not financial advice.

## Annual Prepayment Privileges

Northfield Harbour Lending closed mortgages include the following annual prepayment privileges:

- **Lump sum**: Up to 20% of the original principal balance once per calendar year, on the mortgage anniversary date.
- **Payment increase**: Increase regular payment amount by up to 20% once per calendar year.

These privileges are non-cumulative. Unused amounts do not carry forward to the next year.

## Open Mortgages

Open mortgages may be repaid in full at any time without penalty.

## Prepayment Penalty — Fixed Rate

If you break a fixed-rate closed mortgage early, the penalty is the greater of:
1. 3 months' interest at the contract rate, or
2. The Interest Rate Differential (IRD)

**IRD Calculation**: The difference between your contract rate and the current posted rate for a term closest to your remaining term, multiplied by your outstanding balance and remaining term in years.

## Prepayment Penalty — Variable Rate

Variable rate mortgage breakage penalty is 3 months' interest at the current prime rate.

## Partial Prepayment

Partial prepayments exceeding the annual privilege will incur a penalty calculated on the excess amount only.

## Port and Blend

If you sell your property and purchase another, you may port your existing mortgage to the new property, blending your existing rate with any new funds required.
