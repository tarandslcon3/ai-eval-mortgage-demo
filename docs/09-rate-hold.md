# Rate Hold Policy

SYNTHETIC — fictional lender for evaluation demo. Not financial advice.

## Rate Hold

Northfield Harbour Lending offers a rate hold of up to 120 days from the date of a fully completed pre-approval application.

## What Is Included in a Rate Hold

- The quoted interest rate is locked for the hold period
- The rate hold applies to the specific product and term quoted
- If rates decrease during the hold period, Northfield Harbour Lending will honour the lower of the held rate or the current posted rate at time of funding

## Conditions

- A rate hold requires a signed pre-approval agreement and completed application
- Rate holds are non-transferable to a different property
- Rate holds may be cancelled if a material change occurs in the borrower's financial situation (job loss, significant new debt, credit score drop of 40+ points)
- Rate holds expire automatically at the 120-day mark

## Extension

If your closing date falls after the rate hold expires, you must re-apply. A new rate hold will be issued at the then-current rates with no penalty.

## Qualifying Requirements During Rate Hold

All held rates are still subject to the Northfield Harbour qualifying floor rate of 6.75%. Borrowers must qualify at the higher of their contract rate plus 2.25% or the floor rate.

## Rate Hold vs. Pre-Approval

A rate hold guarantees the rate only — it is not a commitment to lend. Final approval is subject to property appraisal, updated income verification, and satisfactory review of all conditions.
