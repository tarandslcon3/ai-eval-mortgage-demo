# Privacy Policy

SYNTHETIC — fictional lender for evaluation demo. Not financial advice.

## Information We Collect

Northfield Harbour Lending collects personal information needed to process your mortgage application and service your account, including:

- Full legal name, date of birth, SIN (Social Insurance Number)
- Employment information, income details, and tax documents
- Credit bureau reports
- Property information and appraisal details

## How We Use Your Information

Your information is used to:
- Assess your mortgage application
- Verify your identity under federal anti-money laundering (AML) requirements
- Service and administer your mortgage
- Meet regulatory reporting obligations

## Information Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share it with:
- Credit bureaus (Equifax, TransUnion) for credit reporting
- CMHC or mortgage insurers (if your mortgage is insured)
- Legal, appraisal, and title professionals involved in your transaction
- Regulators as required by law

## Retention

We retain your personal information for 7 years following the discharge of your mortgage, or longer if required by law.

## Your Rights

You have the right to:
- Request access to your personal information
- Request corrections to inaccurate information
- Withdraw consent for non-essential uses (may affect your ability to obtain a mortgage)
