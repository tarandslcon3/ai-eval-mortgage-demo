## How to File a Complaint

Northfield Harbour Lending is committed to resolving customer concerns promptly. To file a complaint:

1. **Contact your mortgage advisor directly** — most issues are resolved at this stage within 2 business days.
2. **Escalate to our Client Relations team** — if your advisor cannot resolve the issue, email clientrelations@northfieldharbour.example or call 1-800-555-0192 (Monday to Friday, 8 a.m. to 6 p.m. ET).
3. **Request a formal review** — if you are not satisfied after step 2, submit a written complaint to our Complaints Officer at the address below.

## External Escalation

If you are not satisfied with our resolution, you may escalate to:
- **OBSI (Ombudsman for Banking Services and Investments)**: An independent, free dispute resolution service.
- **FCAC (Financial Consumer Agency of Canada)**: For complaints about violations of consumer protection regulations.

Note: Northfield Harbour Lending is a fictional entity and these external bodies cannot process complaints about it.

## No Retaliation

Filing a complaint will not affect your mortgage terms, interest rate, or renewal options.
