# Mortgage Renewal

SYNTHETIC — fictional lender for evaluation demo. Not financial advice.

## Renewal Process

Northfield Harbour Lending will send a renewal offer 120 days before your mortgage maturity date. Offers are sent by mail and email.

## Early Renewal

You may renew your mortgage up to 120 days before the maturity date without penalty, provided the renewal is at Northfield Harbour Lending and at the current rates on the renewal date.

## Renewal Terms Available

- 6-month open
- 1-year fixed
- 2-year fixed
- 3-year fixed
- 4-year fixed
- 5-year fixed
- 5-year variable

All terms are subject to availability and prevailing rate conditions.

## Renewal Credit Check

A credit check may be conducted at renewal if:
- The borrower is requesting an increase in the loan amount
- There have been missed payments in the prior term
- The mortgage has been in arrears at any point in the prior 12 months

Standard renewals at the same amount with a good payment history do not require re-qualification.

## Transfer-In Mortgages

Borrowers transferring a mortgage from another lender to Northfield Harbour Lending at maturity must complete full underwriting, including income verification and a credit check. No appraisal is required if the loan-to-value ratio is below 65%.

## Missed Renewal

If no renewal is signed by the maturity date, the mortgage converts to a 6-month open mortgage at the current posted open rate.
